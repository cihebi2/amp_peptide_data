MATERIALS AND METHODS

Antimicrobial activity of YZ462 in cellular models

RAW264.7 cell was culture in 12-well plate (1×106/well) for 24h.
Exponentially growing S. aureus MRS153 was harvested and washed three
times with DMEM medium. Resuspended S. aureus MRS153 was used for
infecting RAW264.7 cell for 3 h. Then the cells were washed three times
with DMEM medium. Cells were incubated in 37℃with 5% CO2, YZ462
(8×MIC) was added as the experiment groups, isometric DMSO was added as
the control groups. RAW264.7 cells were lysed with RIPA buffer every 30
min used for CFU determined.

PI Staining Analysis in Eukaryotic cell line

HEK293T and Hela cells were treated with YZ462 and DMSO for 24 h at 37
°C and 5% CO2. PI (5 μg/mL) were added incubated at dark for 20 min,
the cells were washed three times with DMEM medium before flow
cytometry. 

ITC Assay

To test the interaction between PE or CL and compound YZ462, a
calorimetric assay was performed by PEAQ-ITC at 25℃. PE was dissolved
in 10% DMSO (2.0 mM) or CL was dissolved in EtOH (2.0 mM), which was
sequentially injected into the calorimetric cells filled with compound
YZ462 (0.2 mM), which was dissolved in the same buffer, and the
injection was repeated 19 times with an equilibrium interval of 200 s.
The equilibrium dissociation constant (Kd) were obtained employing the
relative software with the instrument.

1-(benzyloxy)-4-bromo-2-nitrobenzene (3). A mixture compounds
4-bromo-2-nitrophenol (327.0 mg, 1.5 mmol) and K2CO3 (618.0 mg, 4.5

(0.53 mL, 4.5 mmol) was dropwise, the mixture was stirred at 60 ℃ for
1 h. After the reaction was complete detected by TLC, the mixture was
extracted with EA and H2O, and the separated organic layer was washed
with brine, dried over Na2SO4, concentrated, and then was purified by
column chromatography to give compound 3 (427.8 mg, 92.6% yield).

3-(4-(benzyloxy)-3-nitrophenyl)thiophene (4). A mixture of compound 3
(427.8 mg, 1.39 mmol) in 1,2-dimethoxyethane (5.0 mL) and anhydrous
ethanol (8.0 mL), catalytic Pd(PPh3)4 was added at r.t., and then the
vial was purged with Ar and stirred for 5 min. Subsequently, the
3-thiopheneboronic acid (355.6 mg, 2.78 mmol) and aqueous solution of
Na2CO3 (2.0 mol/L, 10 mL) were then added to the mixture, and the vial
was purged with Ar again, and the resulting mixture was heated to 95 °C
for overnight. The residue was concentrated and the extracted with
CH2Cl2 and H2O, and the separated organic layer was washed with brine,
dried over Na2SO4, and was purified by column chromatography to give
compound 4 (399.3 mg, 88.5% yield).

2-(benzyloxy)-5-(thiophen-3-yl)aniline (5). A mixture of compound 4
(399.3 mg, 1.28 mmol) in EtOH/H2O= 4:1 (10 mL) was added Fe (645.1 mg,
11.52 mmol) and NH4Cl (890.2 mg, 16.64 mmol), the reaction mixture was
heated at 85 ℃ for 2 h, and then EtOH was evaporated under vacuum. The
residue was extracted with ethyl acetate (EA) and H2O, and the separated
organic layer was washed with brine, dried over Na2SO4, and evaporated
to dryness to give crude compound 5 (296.4 mg, 82.3% yield).

N-(benzo[b]thiophen-2-ylmethyl)-2-(benzyloxy)-5-(thiophen-3-yl)aniline
(6). To a solution of 1-benzothiophen-2-ylmethanol (456.45 mg, 3.0 mmol)

was extracted with CH2Cl2 and H2O, and the separated organic layer was
washed with brine, dried over Na2SO4, concentrated, and then was
purified by column chromatography to give compound 6 (330.9 mg, 73.2%
yield).

N-(benzo[b]thiophen-2-ylmethyl)-N-(2-(benzyloxy)-5-(thiophen-3-yl)phenyl
