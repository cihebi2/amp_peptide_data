Fig. 4. Antibacterial effect of RGD-Pac525 on E. coli KV203 infections in ovo. (a) Embryo mortality (%)

and (b) chick weight (g) after 48 h of E. coli KV203 infection (n>5), compared to that of infected chickens
treated with RGD-Pac525 (n>5), and of uninfected chicks (n =3). (c) Stereomicroscope images of the top and
bottom sides of E. coli KV203 infection on the CAM treated with RGD-Pac525 for 48 h, compared to that of
untreated control. Scale bar =50 mm. (d) Quantification of vascular density (%) using the Image] Vessel tool.
(e) Fluorescent imaging of E. coli KV203 infection in the CAM and distal CAM. Scale bar = 200 um. (f) E. coli
KV203 recovered from brain, liver, and intestine (n> 5) after 48 h of infection on the CAM. Data represent
mean + SEM. (*p<0.05, **p<0.01, ***p< 0.001)
